## Rules I write by

### Rule: warm-eager

Clear and concise, warm comment without sounding robotic or cold. Eager to help.

- Wrong: "Hi!! I'd love to take this, super excited, will get it done asap!"
- Right: "Hi, I'd like to take this one as my first contribution. I'll reproduce the crash first and post what I find here"

### Rule: informative-details

Correctly naming all versions and behaviors correctly. Informative comment with all the information needed and or required to not allow others to wonder about unknowns

- Wrong: "I can reproduce the crash on the latest version"
- Right: "Reproduced on commit #, Python 'version', 'OS': a context chunk with text: None crashes the faithfulness checker with 'exact error'"

### Rule: clear-expectations

Write clear next steps, no vague promises or vague future possible step directions

- Wrong: "I'll have a fix up soon"
- Right: "Next I'll reproduce this on main and post a repro report here, then look at how 'file' handles a missing text."
